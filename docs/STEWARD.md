# Steward — canonical V1

**Locked September 11, 2026. Track: Good Neighbor Agents.**

Steward is an autonomous neighborhood operations agent. It listens to authorized community signals and public service data, investigates whether a physical issue exists, reconciles conflicting evidence, determines responsibility, and dispatches approved supplemental providers when policy allows. It owns the unresolved condition through verification and simulated settlement, surfacing exceptions for human judgment.

**Steward manages reality, not tickets.** A closed government record or a contractor's Done button does not establish physical resolution.

## The one proof

One dumped couch near 1530 S Michigan Ave moves from weak signal to verified resolution. The three mandatory judgments are:

1. **Wait:** first signal scores 65, below the actionable threshold of 70.
2. **Dispute:** corroboration raises the score to 85; a matching completed service record conflicts with newer physical evidence, so the issue stays alive.
3. **Block payment:** partial cleanup scores 90 against 95 required. An operator requests rework; fresh complete proof scores 100, permitting a simulated $72 settlement and resolution.

The matching service record can subsequently add 15 evidence points (85 → 100). The demo must distinguish the pre-lookup score from the post-lookup score. A service match corroborates the identity/history of the issue, not its current resolution.

## Exactly three inputs

- South Loop Neighbors feed, labeled **Simulated opt-in community channel**.
- Direct resident report through a real web input.
- Chicago 311/public service lookup: live where practical, with a clearly labeled fixture/fallback and provenance.

Every input becomes a Signal first. Independent observations link to a canonical Issue representing a physical condition. Duplicate reposts are not independent corroboration.

## Authorized work

The South Loop Demo District supplies a service area, explicit operating policy, seeded budget, and three approved vendors: South Loop Services, Windy City Maintenance, and Lakefront Clean Team.

Autonomous categories: litter, bulky waste, approved graffiti removal. Route potholes, streetlights, and traffic signals to city responsibility. Never dispatch electrical, structural, or hazardous-material work. Unknown responsibility or unresolved authority goes to review. A demo district does not claim real municipal authorization.

Bulky waste contract price: $60 base + $12 large object = $72. The model selects the service; code calculates the rate. Provider eligibility includes approved category, service area, equipment, availability, and verified-insurance fixture. The agent selects among eligible providers using distance, availability, workload, and basic performance.

## Product surfaces

- **Operations Board:** South Loop Demo District, remaining budget, watching/active/review/resolved counts, map, and prominent attention count.
- **Issue Detail:** source evidence and the auditable decision timeline; this gets the most polish.
- **Operator Inbox:** genuine exceptions, failed requirements, request completion, and manual inspection. Exception approval is allowed only if an explicit policy grants it; otherwise omit or disable it in V1.
- **Minimal Crew Form:** accept, check in, upload before/after proof, submit. No separate workforce product.
