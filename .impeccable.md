## Design Context

### Users

District operators need to understand unresolved physical conditions, inspect evidence, and act on exceptions. Assigned crews use job instructions and proof forms, often on a phone. Residents submit observations with minimal administrative knowledge. The hackathon uses explicitly labeled demo personas; government-facing appearance must not imply official government ownership or endorsement.

### Brand Personality

The user wants a professional interface that a government agency would plausibly use, with modern design and ambitious frontend craftsmanship. Three guiding words: **credible, clear, contemporary**. The intended feeling is calm competence: the user can tell what is known, what is unresolved, and what action is needed.

### Aesthetic Direction

The user selected **both themes, with light as the default**. The restored original study uses warm neutral surfaces, deep navy text, restrained teal actions, fine rules and precise spacing, paired with a readable dark theme. Use bundled Public Sans for working text and Newsreader for editorial headings, substantial paired evidence images, and a narrow work-order column. Preserve readable contrast when adapting the study's exact colors.

USWDS [design principles](https://designsystem.digital.gov/design-principles/) inform clarity, trust and accessibility. This is an inspiration reference, not a compliance claim or a commitment to use the USWDS component package.

Use compact navigation, substantial real evidence images, readable rows and timelines, and a clear primary action. Issue Detail is the highest-polish page. Keep visual novelty in composition, typography and interaction quality; do not change the product's actors, authority or required flow to support decoration.

### Design Principles

1. **Evidence leads.** Keep the physical condition, photo comparison, failed requirement and provenance visible. Scores are policy points, never confidence percentages.
2. **One clear next action.** Each exception identifies the current actor and allowed action. Preserve every role restriction; the operator cannot force payment or closure.
3. **Status must be earned.** Acknowledgment follows persistence. No optimistic Paid, Verified, Resolved, or receipt state before the server confirms it. Presentation-only feedback can be immediate.
4. **Professional through restraint.** Use hierarchy, alignment, space and fine dividers. Avoid decorative metrics, generic repeated cards, neon gradients, fabricated seals and chat-centric scaffolding.
5. **Complete on small screens.** Preserve evidence and decisions on mobile, with clear labels, keyboard focus, comfortable touch targets, and reduced-motion support. Color always has a text equivalent.

The original visual study is presentation-only, uses synthetic images and simulated case states, and never calls the service or model. The restored application consumes real API evidence, timestamps, policy results and allowed actions; never copy hard-coded study states into product behavior. React/TypeScript, Vite, Leaflet/OpenStreetMap and FastAPI delivery remain the selected application stack.
